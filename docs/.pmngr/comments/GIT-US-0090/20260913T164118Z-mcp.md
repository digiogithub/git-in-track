The three REST routes landed in `internal/server/youtrackkbapi.go`: `GET /api/v1/youtrack/kb/status`, `POST …/kb/publish` and `POST …/kb/pull`, thin `s.call` wrappers over the vault methods with no sync logic of their own.

Two decisions for a reviewer. The handlers are defined once and mounted twice — under `/youtrack/kb/…` with the `?key=` convention of that subtree, and inside `mountKB`, which gives the per-project and per-team scopes for free — so the two spellings cannot drift. And `remote` is never defaulted on by the route, because a tree view would otherwise become one article read per page. A project with no `integrations.youtrack` block answers 404 on all three.

GIT-T-0215 (the route and conflict-event documentation) is still `in_review` with another agent; `docs/07` §5.5 already carries the routes.
