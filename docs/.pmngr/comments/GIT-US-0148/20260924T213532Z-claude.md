Implemented in the jj change on top of the Phase 11 stack. The rule:

- A touched (tier 1 or 2) `passing` requirement is `suspect` unless its coverage row's new
  `commit` (docs/03 R-REQ-12a rule 6) equals the diff's **head commit**. A passing row carries
  `commit` when every matched result was ingested at one and the same commit (none without a
  commit), or when the stamp decides and `verified.rev` is the current block rev.
- Head commit: `--head <rev>` resolved to its commit; for the working tree, `HEAD` (what
  `spec ingest` records by default) only while the tree differs from `HEAD` in `.pmngr/`
  backlog files at most. Any other uncommitted or untracked file: no head commit, stays suspect.
- `failing` wins and is never flagged suspect; a pending MODIFIED Spec Delta clears nothing
  on its own — it clears when its linked tests pass at head, like any other requirement.
- No clock is read: same diff, history, tree and ingested results give the same answer.

Tests: `TestImpactSuspectAtHead` (14 cases), a new golden `impact_reverified.golden.json`,
`TestClassifyCommit`, and the end-to-end `TestSpecImpactGateClearsAtHead` (real git repo:
not re-run trips the gate, re-run at head passes, failing re-run trips as failing).
