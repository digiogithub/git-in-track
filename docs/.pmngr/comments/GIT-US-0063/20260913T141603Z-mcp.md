Landed in `internal/syncengine/` (new package, standard library only): `Engine` with `New`/`Register`/`Start`/`Enqueue`/`Cancel`/`Flush`/`Close`/`Pending`/`Snapshot`, keyed debounced batching copied from `gitops.Committer`, a resizable worker pool over one `sync.Cond`, and one shared token-bucket limiter every worker passes through. `go build ./...`, `go vet`, `gofmt`, `golangci-lint` and `go test -race -count=6 ./internal/syncengine/...` are all clean; the suite has no wall-clock sleeps and runs in about a second.

Two things a reviewer must know. First, the lifecycle gained a `Start(ctx)` between `New` and the first `Enqueue`, because the journal of GIT-US-0070 is replayed there and replayed jobs must find their handlers already registered; `Enqueue` before `Start` is accepted and simply waits. Second, fencing is two-layered: `Engine.generation` retires the pool on `SetWorkers`/`Close`, but a worker already inside a handler always finishes its batch — dropping it would lose jobs — while per-result fencing lives on the job's own stamp, so cancelling a job mid-flight makes the worker's outcome be discarded rather than applied. The state map carries two extra re-queue edges (`running → queued`, `failed → queued`) that GIT-US-0067 requires; they are documented on the map and in the story body, and everything else is refused.
