Landed on `feat/phase-9-agent-and-search` (web only; not committed by me).

**Types** (`web/src/api/provider.ts`) — `SearchHit` is now defined in `provider.ts` instead of being re-exported from `@/core-bridge/api` (the core one keeps its required `snippet`/`score` for the WASM bridge): `{kind: 'item'|'page'; id?; path?; title; snippet?; score?; project?; vaultId?; source: 'core'|'pando'}`. New `SearchResult = {hits: SearchHit[]; degraded?: boolean}` and `search(query): Promise<SearchResult>`. `Capabilities.fullTextSearch` widened to `'core'|'bleve'|'pando'`.

**Providers** — `companion-provider.ts`: new `toSearchResult`; `toSearchHits` now accepts the `{hits, degraded}` envelope as well as the old `{results}` and bare-array shapes, and a hit with no/unknown `source` reads as `'core'` (backwards compatible). `toFullTextSearch` maps `features.search === 'pando'`. `browser-provider.ts` stamps every hit `'core'` and never reports degraded. `fake-provider.ts` gained `FakeData.search = {fullTextSearch?, semantic?, degraded?}` plus the mutable `semanticHits`/`searchDegraded`, appending scripted semantic hits after the exact ones and skipping duplicates.

**UI** (`WorkspaceSearch.tsx`) — two groups: "Exact matches" (list label unchanged: "Search results") then "Related by meaning", rendered only when the capability is `'pando'` and the pref is on; no empty heading otherwise. Semantic rows show the title, a subdued relevance (`82% match`, or `score N.N` above 1) and the why-matched snippet: split into text nodes with `<mark>` around the query terms the passage contains, no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, clamped at 180 chars behind a `aria-expanded` Show more/less button. A `degraded` response adds an unobtrusive "Semantic search unavailable — exact matches only." note. Debounce (`useDeferredValue`) and the two-character minimum are untouched.

**Preference** — `web/src/app/ui-prefs.ts`: `semanticResults: boolean` (default `true`) + `setSemanticResults`, persisted with the other layout settings in `localStorage` under `gintrack:ui-prefs`.

**Tests** — `WorkspaceSearch.test.tsx` (grouping + document order, capability gate, highlighting, `<script>` in a snippet rendered as text, expand control, toggle hides/persists, degraded note), new `src/api/search-provider.test.ts` (envelope/legacy-shape mapping, fake semantic scripting) and `src/app/ui-prefs.test.ts` (default, persistence across a module reload). Updated the search assertions in `companion-provider.test.ts` and `browser-provider.test.ts`.

Green: `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx vitest run src/api src/features/workspace src/app` (21 files, 250 tests), `eslint` and `prettier --check` on every touched file.
